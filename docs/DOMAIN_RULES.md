# AROH Domain Rules

## Well

A drilling well with:

- geographic location
- trajectory
- depth
- geological formations
- drilling parameters
- historical events

---

# Offset Well

A previously drilled well that may provide
historical information relevant to the active well.

---

# Analogue Well

An offset/historical well that has meaningful
similarity to the active well.

Distance alone does not determine analogy.

---

# Historical Event

An operational or drilling event associated with:

- a well
- depth
- formation
- time
- event type
- severity
- mitigation
- outcome

---

# Risk

A potential future operational issue inferred
from historical patterns and/or current data.

Risk is not certainty.

---

# Evidence

Information supporting a prediction or explanation.

Evidence should identify its source whenever possible.

---

# Decision Support

AROH provides information to assist qualified
personnel.

It does not autonomously control drilling operations.
